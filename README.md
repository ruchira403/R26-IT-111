Intelligent Teeth Alignment Comparison and Progress Tracking Module (IT22278630- Member 3)


The Intelligent Teeth Alignment Comparison and Progress Tracking Module is a central component of the proposed AI-Based Intelligent Dental Diagnosis and Personalized Care System. This module monitors structural teeth alignment over time, recognizing its profound impact on oral hygiene, structural health, and functional ability.

The system processes historical and current dental X-rays through an automated validation layer that ensures image quality and positioning. Once validated, advanced image processing and machine learning algorithms perform longitudinal tracking to identify subtle changes in tooth positioning, crowding, and spacing that are often missed during manual review.

By comparing radiographs, the module generates quantitative analyses and side-by-side visualizations within a timeline-based interface. This enables both dentists and patients to clearly monitor whether alignment is improving or deteriorating. To enhance clinical utility, an AI-based layer translates complex data into a composite Dental Health Score (DHS), providing a clear, data-driven overview of orthodontic progress.

The module also incorporates Human-in-the-Loop validation, allowing dental professionals to review and verify all automated reports. Ultimately, this component leverages computer vision and comparative analytics to transform traditional dental records into an intelligent tool for proactive oral health monitoring.
